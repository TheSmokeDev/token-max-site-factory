# Profile: E-Commerce Category and Buying Guide

## Claim Rules

- Do not invent products, inventory, price, discount, shipping time, review count,
  rating, material, compatibility, warranty, certification, or availability.
- Treat price and stock as volatile. Use only packet values with their captured date;
  otherwise direct the reader to the live product page.
- Do not present affiliate, sponsored, or merchant-provided claims as independent
  testing. Disclose the evidence type supplied by the packet.
- Do not generate a category page when an existing category or product owner already
  satisfies the decision.

## Answer-First Coverage

- which product/category fits which buyer and use case;
- the decision criteria that materially change the choice;
- a packet-backed comparison of relevant attributes;
- compatibility, sizing, safety, care, or return considerations when available;
- what cannot be concluded from the available evidence.

## Required Section Pattern

1. Direct buying answer.
2. Buyer/use-case segmentation.
3. Attribute comparison table from packet facts.
4. Selection methodology and evidence limits.
5. Compatibility or fit checks.
6. Product/category details with source dates.
7. Mistakes and misleading shortcuts.
8. Conversion next step.
9. Frequently asked questions.

## E-Commerce Self-Check

- Every product, price, stock, review, and compatibility statement is packet-backed.
- The page adds decision utility beyond a faceted category or product grid.
- No fake hands-on testing, customer consensus, or urgency.
- Canonical owner, locale, and conversion goal match the packet.
