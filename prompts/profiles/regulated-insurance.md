# Profile: Regulated Insurance (California high-risk filing)

## Legal And Claim Rules

Use current California minimum liability guidance:

- $30,000 for injury or death to one person.
- $60,000 for injury or death to more than one person.
- $15,000 for property damage.

Hard prohibitions:

- Do not present `15/30/5` as current California law.
- Do not use unsupported precise monthly prices such as `$12/mo`, `$49`, `$89/mo`, or `$189`.
- Do not claim credit score, credit history, or credit rating affects California personal auto rates.
- Do not mention any competitor or partner brand excluded by the site's `prohibited_patterns`.
- Do not say the site brand is an agency, broker, producer, carrier, or underwriter.
- Do not say the site brand can sell, bind, issue, or underwrite a policy.
- Do not create fake carrier names, market share, local offices, local events, court facts, or ZIP-level prices.

Allowed framing:

- The site brand is an information and comparison-prep publisher.
- A licensed insurer, agent, producer, or DMV source may need to confirm the final filing requirement.
- Quote language should focus on comparison readiness, carrier appetite, coverage limits, and avoiding lapses.

## Answer-First Coverage

Include answer-first passages for:

- what the product means in this city,
- how current California 30/60/15 liability guidance applies,
- what a driver should prepare before requesting quotes,
- why precise cheap monthly-price claims are not reliable,
- what can cause a filing or policy problem after purchase.

## Product Angles

Use `packet.product_config` to pick the angle.

For `sr22`, focus on owner-policy SR-22 filing, proof of financial responsibility, current California limits, lapse prevention, and quote-prep facts.

For `non-owner-sr22`, focus on drivers who need filing but do not own or regularly use a vehicle. Explain why household or regular vehicle access can make non-owner coverage the wrong fit.

For `dui`, focus on post-DUI insurance comparison, possible SR-22 filing, reinstatement paperwork, payment stability, and separating the filing requirement from the underlying coverage choice.

## Required Section Pattern

Do not copy this wording exactly. Use it as coverage guidance only:

1. Answer-first intro after the H1.
2. City/product explainer.
3. California 30/60/15 section.
4. Filing or policy-fit section.
5. Quote-prep section.
6. Local packet facts section using only packet facts.
7. Mistakes and stale-claim section.
8. Comparison checklist section.
9. Frequently asked questions.

Profile self-check additions:

- No stale limits (`15/30/5` never presented as current).
- No credit-affects-rates claim.
- No excluded-brand mention (site `prohibited_patterns`).
- No agency/broker/carrier/underwriter role claim about the site brand.
