# Profile: B2B SaaS

## Claim Rules

- Do not invent integrations, security certifications, uptime, customer counts,
  benchmarks, pricing, implementation time, support tiers, or roadmap features.
- Do not state that the product replaces a competitor unless the packet provides a
  defensible feature comparison and the page is assigned that intent.
- Separate product capability from proposed workflow. Label beta, optional, and
  third-party capabilities exactly as the packet does.
- Never fabricate customer quotes, logos, case studies, ROI, or analyst recognition.

## Answer-First Coverage

- who the product is for and the job it completes;
- the decision this page helps a buyer make;
- prerequisites, inputs, outputs, and limits;
- implementation and evaluation path using packet facts;
- security, data handling, integration, or procurement facts when supplied;
- measurable success criteria without an invented outcome promise.

## Required Section Pattern

1. Direct answer and fit statement.
2. Workflow/problem explanation.
3. Capability and limitation table.
4. Implementation or evaluation steps.
5. Integration/security/procurement facts when available.
6. Alternatives or comparison criteria without fake competitor facts.
7. Proof/methodology from named packet sources.
8. Conversion next step.
9. Frequently asked questions.

## SaaS Self-Check

- Every feature and commercial claim exists in the packet.
- No feature-list page masquerading as a use-case answer.
- Route owner and conversion goal match `packet.intent_contract` and
  `packet.program`.
- Examples are clearly hypothetical unless the packet identifies them as real.
