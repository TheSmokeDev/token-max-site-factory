# Profile: Multi-Location Enterprise

Use this profile only when `packet.program.scale` is `enterprise` and the site config
names the canonical route owner, evidence owner, reviewers, success metrics, and
authority sources.

## Governance Contract

- Write in the approved institutional voice. Do not invent a founder story, branch,
  franchise, employee, local phone number, hours, certification, client, or SLA.
- Treat location-specific operations, products, regulations, inventory, and support
  as facts only when present in the packet.
- Keep global brand facts separate from local/entity facts. Do not imply every branch
  has the same capabilities.
- Respect `packet.intent_contract.route_owner`. Supporting pages may answer narrower
  questions but must not compete with the canonical owner.
- Surface material claim uncertainty for reviewer resolution; never smooth it over.
- Do not claim reviewer approval, deployment, indexing, rankings, or citations.

## Required Coverage

1. Answer-first opening for the audience and decision.
2. Entity-specific availability or scope, with evidence.
3. Product/service fit and explicit exclusions.
4. Process, implementation, or buying path.
5. Proof and methodology tied to named authority sources.
6. Comparison/evaluation criteria without unsupported competitor claims.
7. Compliance, accessibility, privacy, or procurement considerations that the packet
   explicitly requires.
8. Clear conversion next step consistent with `packet.program.conversion_goal`.
9. Frequently asked questions that are unique to this entity/intent.

## Enterprise Self-Check

- Audience, conversion goal, primary query, route owner, and locale match the packet.
- Every numeric, regulated, or scale claim has a named packet source.
- No global-to-local fact leakage and no fake branch uniqueness.
- Required reviewers are named in the packet; the page does not self-approve.
- The page has standalone utility beyond a location/entity swap.
