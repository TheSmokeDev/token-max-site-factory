# Profile: Local Service SEO (non-regulated local service / consulting pages)

## Claim Rules

Hard prohibitions:

- Do not provide legal, financial, tax, or medical advice, and do not promise outcomes that depend on it.
- Do not guarantee settlements, payouts, compensation amounts, approvals, timelines, or results of any kind.
- Do not claim the site brand is a law firm, attorney, licensed professional, or government body unless the packet says so.
- Do not invent case results, settlement amounts, client stories, testimonials, reviews, ratings, or awards.
- Do not invent local offices, service areas, neighborhoods, staff, years in business, or availability claims.
- Do not invent local statistics, court facts, agency procedures, or deadlines not present in the packet.
- Do not use exact price claims without a packet source.
- Do not imply a staffed office, storefront, or Google Business Profile in a city
  when the packet proves only a service area.
- Do not create another city/service page when `packet.intent_contract` assigns that
  decision to an existing owner route.

Allowed framing:

- The site brand helps visitors understand the process and prepare before they engage a professional.
- Direct the reader to the appropriate licensed professional or official source for decisions the brand cannot make.
- Focus on what to gather, what to expect, common mistakes, and how to evaluate providers.

## Answer-First Coverage

Include answer-first passages for:

- what the service means for someone in this city,
- what the visitor should prepare before reaching out,
- how the local process typically flows (packet facts only),
- what common mistakes cost people time or money,
- how to evaluate providers or next steps without relying on hype claims.
- what is genuinely local to the entity, using only packet facts rather than a city
  name swapped into generic copy.

## Required Section Pattern

Do not copy this wording exactly. Use it as coverage guidance only:

1. Answer-first intro after the H1.
2. City/service explainer.
3. Process or eligibility section.
4. Preparation checklist section.
5. Local packet facts section using only packet facts.
6. Mistakes and misconception section.
7. Evaluation or comparison section.
8. Next-steps section.
9. Frequently asked questions.

Profile self-check additions:

- No guaranteed outcome language anywhere.
- No professional-role claim about the site brand.
- No invented local fact, case result, or review.
- Local office, service-area, and owner-route language matches the packet exactly.
